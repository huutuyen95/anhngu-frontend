Last summer my family went to Da Nang for five days. We travelled by plane and it was my first flight, so I was very excited. Every morning I swim in the sea with my younger brother and in the afternoon we visited some famous places such as Ba Na Hills and the Dragon Bridge.

Festivals around the world
Every country has its own special festivals. In Vietnam, the most important one is Tet, the Lunar New Year. Families clean their houses, buy flowers and cook traditional food such as banh chung. Children receive lucky money in red envelopes and wish their grandparents good health.

In Japan, people celebrate Hanami in spring. They sit under cherry trees, eat lunch with friends and enjoy the beautiful pink flowers. The festival only lasts about two weeks because the flowers fall very quickly.

In Brazil, the Carnival in Rio de Janeiro is famous all over the world. Thousands of dancers wear colourful clothes and move through the streets to the sound of samba music. Visitors come from many countries to watch the parade.

Although these festivals are different, they all bring people together. They help young people learn about their culture and give families a chance to spend time with each other.
